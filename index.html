02

About

Faisal Ahmed is a researcher at NVISION Systems and Technologies SL (INTERA Group) in Barcelona and an Assistant Professor (on study leave) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Premier University, Bangladesh. He earned his PhD in Computer Science from Universitat Rovira i Virgili in November 2026, graduating Excellent cum laude.

His doctoral research, supervised by Prof. Josep Domingo-Ferrer, Prof. David Sánchez and Dr. Zouhair Haddi, investigates privacy and generalization in federated learning for multi-institutional clinical AI. He completed an MSCA doctoral fellowship under BosomShield and research visits at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, the University of Pisa and the University of Maribor.

He holds an MSc in Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science) from United International University (2022) and a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Chittagong (2017). His interests include trustworthy machine learning, medical image analysis, explainable AI and privacy-enhancing technologies.

03

Research

01

Federated learning for clinical AI

Learning across hospitals and institutions while keeping sensitive data local, with emphasis on generalisation under non-IID data and institutional heterogeneity.

Key themes: multi-centre learning · clinical validation · domain shift

02

Privacy-enhancing technologies

Understanding and improving privacy in distributed machine learning through privacy-preserving architectures, privacy assessment and practical protection mechanisms.

Key themes: privacy–utility trade-offs · secure collaboration · data protection

03

Trustworthy and explainable AI

Developing AI systems whose performance, reliability and decisions can be assessed, interpreted and communicated in high-impact settings.

Key themes: explainability · robustness · fairness · reliability

04

Explainable and collaborative AI

Studying privacy-preserving explainability, collaborative Shapley value learning and federated models for biomedical text and clinical imaging.

Key themes: explainable FL · Shapley values · biomedical NLP
